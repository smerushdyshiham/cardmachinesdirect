Estimates are not quotes

The calculator gives an estimate. It uses the takings and average sale you enter, an assumed split of 90% debit and 10% credit cards, and prices checked on the date shown next to the results. Your real costs depend on your actual card mix, your business type and each provider's own checks, so your written quote may be higher or lower than the estimate.

Competitor prices come from each provider's own published UK pricing page. They're shown so you can compare. We're not connected with those providers, and their names and trademarks belong to them. Prices can change without notice. If you spot one that's out of date, please tell us.

Your contract is with the provider
