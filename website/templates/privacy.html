Privacy and cookies

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to stop us. In plain English.

This notice needs a final check by whoever handles data protection for the business, including the company name, registered address and ICO registration number, before launch.

Who we are

Card Machines Direct is responsible for the information collected on this site. Contact us about anything on this page at {{ contact_email }}.

When you ask for a quote

We collect what you type into the quote form and any statement you upload. We use it only to work out and send your quote, and to follow up on it. Statements are stored on our own server, are visible only to our team, and are deleted automatically {{ retention_days }} days after you send them, or sooner if you ask.

Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We never sell your details.

The calculator

The numbers you put into the calculator are sent to our server to work out the estimate. We count how the calculator is used, for example which takings band people choose, but we don't link that to your name unless you then ask for a quote.

Visit statistics

We count visits to understand which pages help and where people get stuck: where you arrived from (for example an email we sent), which pages and sections you viewed, how long for, and whether you asked for a quote. This uses a random ID stored in your browser. It isn't used for advertising and isn't shared. {% if analytics_consent_required %}We only count your visits if you choose "That's fine" in the cookie message.{% endif %} Visit statistics are deleted automatically after {{ analytics_days }} days.

Email campaign tracking

When we email business owners, our email software can record whether a link in that email led to a visit. That tracking code only runs on this site if you choose "That's fine" in the cookie message. If you said no, it doesn't load.

How long we keep things

  • Uploaded statements: deleted automatically after {{ retention_days }} days.
  • Quote requests: kept for as long as we need them to deal with your quote and any follow-up. Ask us at any time and we'll delete them.
  • Visit statistics: deleted automatically after {{ analytics_days }} days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by emailing {{ contact_email }}. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
